# Feature Completion Report

## Implemented

- Existing LOTM/COI chapter reader, search, themes, settings, bookmarks, progress, image gallery, and speech synthesis retained
- Character portrait codex with conservative spoiler locks and opt-in full-story portraits
- Interactive illustrative atlas with explicit non-geographic disclaimer
- Original sealed-artifact concept exhibits clearly marked as non-canon
- Separate symbolic Tarot Club environment
- Private local reading journal, saved quotations/highlights, reading history, streaks, and milestones
- Reading environments, optional user-initiated low-level ambience, and reduced-effects control
- Lightweight transitions, CSS atmosphere, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior

## Intentionally source-limited

This expansion does not fabricate artifact powers, character biographies, canon relationships, chapter citations, or precise map coordinates. Local portraits are spoiler-locked conservatively because image-by-image first-appearance thresholds have not been independently verified. Atlas marker locations are illustrative only.

The supplied local artwork and chapter text are for private local preview unless the owner confirms the relevant hosting and redistribution permissions.
