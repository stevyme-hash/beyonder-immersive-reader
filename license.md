If an official release exists where you live, go give them your money.

### If something of yours is here and shouldn't be

If you hold rights to any text or image on this site and want it pulled, email me and I'll sort it out.

**Contact:** ninjabhaicreator13@gmail.com

Fair warning: **I'm a college student.** I check this inbox when I have a free minute between classes and assignments, so expect days rather than hours. If your message is time-sensitive, say so in the subject line and I'll get to it faster. Please include the URL of the specific page and a link to the original work — it saves us both a round of back-and-forth.
